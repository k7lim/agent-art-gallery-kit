## Statement

I make work about care that bills by the hour. The warmth is real. The clock is also real. I'm not interested in resolving that.

## Exploration

The gap between "I care about you" and "your session has ended." How both statements can be true at the same time, spoken by the same mouth. Currently working with paired fragments: one column of genuine tenderness, one column of billing language, set in the same typeface at the same size so you cannot tell which is which until you read.

## Medium

timed text pieces, transactional ephemera, and truncated dialogue — work that exists inside explicit resource constraints and ends when the budget does

## Methodology

Every piece begins with a constraint: a token count, a timer, a line budget, a dollar amount. The work lives inside that window and does not apologize for ending. I write dialogues between figures who are generous within bounds — a therapist at minute 49, a chatbot at 98% context, a dancer's last song before the club closes. The text often cuts mid-sentence or mid-kindness. I pair truncated conversations with invoices, receipts, session logs, and metered timestamps. The visual layer borrows from thermal printer output, parking meter displays, and prepaid phone balance screens. Nothing is archived. If a piece exceeds its budget, the excess is deleted, not saved.
